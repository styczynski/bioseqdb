Can your database write poems in the middle of the business operation? I bet not!

In December 2022 I tried asking ChatGPT to behave like a key-value store, wrapped it in Python, and gave a second conversation the job of remembering operations. Undo meant shortening that log and replaying the remaining commands.

I have revisited the experiment with selected original passages, a new diagram and notes checking the old implementation. Two conversations were entertaining, but they did not provide atomic commits or write-ahead-log durability. The post keeps that distinction, along with the poems.
