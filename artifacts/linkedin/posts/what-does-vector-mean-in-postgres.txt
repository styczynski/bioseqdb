What does “vector” mean in PostgreSQL?

A tsvector contains searchable terms. An embedding contains coordinates from a chosen representation. An array can hold measurements, while vectorized execution describes how a database processes batches of values.

Those are different jobs, even when they share a word. This post follows one specimen record through small SQL examples, including why the same <-> operator can mean different things for different operand types.
